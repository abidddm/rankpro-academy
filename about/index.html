Who we are

Your rank, our mission

We run offline, classroom-based coaching out of Calicut for SSC, Railway (RRB) and Kerala PSC exams. Every batch follows the same structured 12-month program — six months to complete the syllabus, six more to convert it into exam-day performance — supported by experienced faculty, daily practice and dedicated facilities.

Our approach

The RankPro Method

Five phases, run on repeat, until the score holds under exam conditions.

Phase 1

Learn

Complete the syllabus and build concepts from the ground up.

Phase 2

Practice

PYQs, topic tests and regular exercises to make concepts stick.

Phase 3

Test

Full-length mock tests run under real exam-like conditions.

Phase 4

Analyse

Identify mistakes, weak areas and time-management issues.

Phase 5

Improve

Revise, practise, retest — and watch the score move.
